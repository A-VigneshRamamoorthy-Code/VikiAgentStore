Leap VPN: Own the Network

Your Network. Your Rules

Leap VPN builds a private WireGuard VPN in your own Microsoft Azure subscription (or on a PC at home) and controls it from your iPhone and Mac. The developer runs no servers and never sees your traffic.

How it works

  • Sign in with your Microsoft account and pick locations from 40 Azure regions.
  • Connecting starts your server; when no device is connected it is stopped or removed, so Azure stops billing for compute.
  • Economy (the cheapest mode) costs about US$0.015 per connected hour in the lowest-priced region at Azure list prices, plus data transfer, billed by Microsoft to you.
  • An optional DNS filter on your own server blocks ad and tracker domains in browsers and apps.

Subscription

Leap VPN Plus ($0.99/month or $9.99/year, each with a 2-week free trial for new subscribers) is needed to connect after the trial; signing in and setting up your servers are free. One subscription covers iPhone and Mac. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.
