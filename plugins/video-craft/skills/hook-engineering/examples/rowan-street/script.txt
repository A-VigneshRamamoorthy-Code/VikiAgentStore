At three in the morning, one window on Rowan Street was still burning.

It had been burning for nine years.

Nobody knew why... and tonight, for the first time, it went dark.

Maya walked that street home from the hospital every night.
She was a nurse. She noticed the things that stopped.

So she crossed the road, and she knocked.

Nobody answered.
She knocked again... harder.
The rain began. The house stayed black.

Then a lock turned.

An old man opened the door. His name was Tomas.
He would not talk about the lamp.
He said only this. "The house sells in the morning."

Maya stood there... soaked, and foolish, and certain.
She thought about every night she had walked home breaking.
And the one thing on that street that never went dark.

Tomas had a son. The son left after a bad winter.
Tomas promised him a lamp in the window. Always.

Nine years.

Nobody came.

"So I am finished," he said, and he reached for the door.

But Maya put her hand out.
"He is not the only one who saw it."

She told him what a burning window does to a person at three in the morning.
She told him she had never once walked that street alone.

You have passed a light like that. You just never knocked.

Tomas looked at the lamp for a long moment... and he switched it on.

The house sold anyway.

But on Rowan Street, there is still one light at three in the morning.

It is only in a different window now.
