## Description

Leap VPN gives you a private VPN that you own. Instead of sending your traffic through a VPN company's servers, Leap builds WireGuard servers inside your own Microsoft Azure account and connects you to them. Nobody else runs, sees or shares your servers — not even us.

YOUR SERVERS, YOUR RULES
• Sign in with your Microsoft account and pick the locations you want.
• Leap creates the servers in your Azure subscription. You hold the keys.
• Leap has no VPN servers, no accounts and no traffic logs. Your traffic never passes through us.

PAY ONLY WHILE CONNECTED
• A server starts when you connect and stops when you disconnect, so nothing runs while you're off.
• Azure bills you directly for the time you use — from roughly 1.5 US cents an hour for an Economy server in central India (compute, public IP and disk, Azure list prices; your region and currency may differ).
• The Usage screen shows hours, data and estimated cost per location.

40 LOCATIONS
• Choose from 40 Azure locations across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Asia-Pacific.
• Economy or Turbo per location: save money or connect faster.

ADS AND TRACKERS BLOCKED
• Ad and tracker domains are filtered on your own server, for every app on the device, at no extra cost.
• Turn it off per location whenever you like.
• Ads served from the same domains as the content (for example many in-video ads) can't be blocked this way.

TRAVEL WITH YOUR OWN SERVICES
• Connect to a server back home to reach your own subscriptions and services while you're away. Availability of third-party services depends on their own terms and may vary.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No bandwidth caps from us. Azure bills data transfer to your subscription.
• Protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
• One subscription works on iPhone and Mac.

LEAP VPN PLUS
Every feature works from day one. Leap VPN Plus is an optional subscription that supports development: $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, with a 2-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Azure usage is billed separately by Microsoft to your own Azure subscription.
